# Enterprise AI Readiness Playbook

## Overview

Many organizations are investing in Artificial Intelligence, but not all of them are ready to scale it.

In my experience, the biggest challenges are rarely related to AI technology itself. More often, the issues come from fragmented data, unclear ownership, inconsistent business definitions, and a lack of governance.

This repository explores the foundations that help organizations prepare for sustainable AI adoption.

The objective is not to focus on a specific tool or platform. Instead, the focus is on the capabilities that enable people to trust data, make informed decisions, and build AI solutions that deliver real business value.

## Topics Covered

This playbook is organized into a series of short documents.

### 01. What Is AI Readiness?

A practical view of what it means for an organization to be ready for AI and why readiness is about more than technology.

### 02. Common AI Failure Patterns

Lessons learned from common challenges that prevent AI initiatives from moving beyond experimentation.

### 03. Enterprise AI Readiness Framework

A simple framework covering the key capabilities required to support AI adoption at scale.

### 04. Semantic Models and Knowledge Graphs

Why business context matters and how semantic models and knowledge graphs help create a shared understanding of information.

### 05. 90-Day Readiness Assessment

A structured approach for assessing current capabilities and identifying improvement opportunities.

---

## The Areas That Matter Most

When assessing AI readiness, I typically focus on six areas:

- Data Foundation
- Governance and Trust
- Metadata and Discoverability
- Semantic Models
- Knowledge Management
- Operating Model and Ownership

These capabilities often have a greater impact on long-term success than the choice of AI platform or model.

## Final Thoughts

Organizations often focus on AI implementation before establishing the foundations required to support it.

The most successful initiatives I have seen are built on trusted data, clear ownership, shared business definitions, and strong governance practices.

AI becomes significantly easier to scale once these fundamentals are in place.
