# Executive Report

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes the pricing strategy for three SKUs (SKU-4089, SKU-9912, and SKU-1043) in our inventory. We assess the current prices, recommended prices, markdown percentages, and margins for each product. Our analysis reveals that the current prices are higher than the recommended prices for two out of three SKUs, indicating opportunities for price adjustments.

## Pricing Action Table

| SKU | Name | Current Price (SEK) | Recommended Price (SEK) | Markdown (%) | Margin (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SKU-4089 | Pro Noise-Canceling Headphones | 2499 | 2155 | 13.8% | 44.3% |
| SKU-9912 | Ergonomic Mesh Office Chair | 4500 | 4116 | 8.5% | 56.3% |
| SKU-1043 | Waterproof Trail Backpack 30L | 899 | 832 | 7.5% | 57.9% |

## Total Capital Recoverable Estimate

Based on the markdown percentages and margins, we estimate the total capital recoverable for a price adjustment. Assuming a uniform markdown percentage across all SKUs, we calculate:

Total Capital Recoverable Estimate = (Markdown Percentage x Current Price) x Margin Percentage

For each SKU:

- SKU-4089: (13.8% x 2499) x 44.3% = 1,444.29 SEK
- SKU-9912: (8.5% x 4500) x 56.3% = 2,511.49 SEK
- SKU-1043: (7.5% x 899) x 57.9% = 595.19 SEK

Total Capital Recoverable Estimate: 1,444.29 + 2,511.49 + 595.19 = 4,550.97 SEK
